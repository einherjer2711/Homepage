Privacy policy

In short: Our games do not collect any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no in-app purchases. Your progress stays on your device.

2. Our apps

This section applies to all pastfive apps, currently Sunny Hill Zoo (German: “Tierpark Sonnenhügel”) and Tribora.

No personal data

The apps do not collect, store or transmit any personal data. They contain no advertising, no analytics or tracking services, no accounts or logins and no purchases.

Game progress

Progress and settings are stored only locally on your device. We have no access to them. Uninstalling the app deletes your progress.

Backups, photos and sharing

With “Back up”, “Photo” or “Certificate” you can save or share files via Android's share menu (e.g. to your gallery or Google Drive). You alone decide where a file goes; we receive nothing.

Internet

Our apps use the internet permission for one thing only: at most once a day (or when you tap “Check for updates”) it downloads a small file from this website containing the latest version number. No data about you or your game is sent. For technical reasons the server – as with any website visit – sees your device's IP address (see section 3). You can turn off the automatic update check in the app's settings. Downloading an update opens your browser.

Other permissions

Sunny Hill Zoo only uses “Vibration” for short haptic feedback in the game (can be turned off). Apart from internet (for the update check), Tribora requests no other permissions.

Children

The apps are made for the whole family. Since no personal data is collected, no data from children is collected either.

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads no content from third-party servers (even the font is hosted on this website).

Contact by email

If you email us, we only use your address and message to reply and delete them once they are no longer needed. Our mailbox is hosted by OVH SAS, 2 rue Kellermann, 59100 Roubaix, France (servers in the EU).

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection – just send us an email. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at.

If anything changes in the apps or on the website that affects privacy, we will update this policy. The current version is always available here.
