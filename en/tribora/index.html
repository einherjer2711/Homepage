Tribora

From campfire to factory

Lead your people from the Stone Age to the Industrial Age!

It all begins with a campfire and a few huts. Gather berries, fell trees and build your first village. Your villagers have names, jobs and stories of their own – and with every age, the little settlement grows into a thriving city.

“Tribora was the first pastfive game – originally built just for me, as a tribute to the great building and strategy classics I used to love.”

What awaits you

  • Six agesStone Age, Bronze Age, Iron Age, Middle Ages, Renaissance and Industrial Age – each with new buildings, tools and inventions.
  • SeasonsAutumn brings the harvest, in winter your people live off their stores – plan ahead!
  • AnimalsHunters stalk deer, sheep graze in the pasture, and dogs and cats move into cosy homes.
  • Wonders of the worldStone circle, pyramid, lighthouse, leaning tower and palace of the arts.
  • Neighbouring tribesTalk to their chiefs, give gifts, make friends – or defend your village.
  • Adventures and trophiesSurvive the harsh winter, settle an island and collect trophies.
