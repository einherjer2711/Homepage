Why pastfive?

Games that are just games.

You know the feeling: a lovely game, you're just getting into it – and suddenly the only way forward is paying real money. Or an ad pops up every few minutes.

That's exactly what annoyed us. So we started making our own games: a building game through the ages, inspired by the strategy classics of old, and a cosy zoo for the little ones in the family.

Our games are the way games used to be: you get them once, and then they're yours. Completely. No in-app purchases, no ads, no collecting your data. They even work without internet.

Because sometimes that's exactly what you need: a break where nothing has to be done. And a game that is simply a game.
