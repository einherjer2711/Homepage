How to install

The app isn't on the Google Play Store (yet) – you get it directly from this website:

  1. Download: Open this page on your phone and tap “Download the app” above. The file Minispiele.apk is saved.
  2. Open: Tap the downloaded file (in the notification or in your “Downloads” folder).
  3. Allow: The first time, Android asks whether your browser may install apps. Tap Settings → Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Install: Tap Install. Done – Minigames is now with your other apps.

Two prompts are normal: The first time, Android asks whether your browser may install apps (step 3). Google Play Protect may also scan the app and show “Unknown app”, because it doesn't come from the Play Store – then tap More details → Install anyway.
If Play Protect explicitly reports the app as harmful, please cancel and send us the message at hello@pastfive.app.

Updates

The app checks at most once a day whether there is a new version and then asks you. You can also check yourself: Menu ☰ → ℹ️ About the game · Updates → 🔄 Check for updates. The automatic check can be turned off in ⚙️ Settings. Simply install the new version over the old one – your progress is kept.
